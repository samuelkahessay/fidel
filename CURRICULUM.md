# Fidel Curriculum

A progressive 5-lesson introduction to Amharic, from first greetings through everyday conversations.

## At a Glance

| | Content | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Lessons | Structured units with grammar, vocabulary, and phrases | 5 |
| Grammar Patterns | Sentence templates with substitution drills | 14 |
| Vocabulary | Words organized by category | 69 |
| Phrases | Essential expressions for daily life | 50 |
| Conversations | Realistic dialogue scenarios | 10 |
| Fidel Script | Ethiopic characters (34 consonants × 7 vowel forms) | 238 |
| Numbers | Counting from 1 to 100 | 19 entries |


## Practice Modes

### Flashcards
Spaced-repetition cards for vocabulary and phrases. The system tracks ease factor, interval, and review dates using a modified SM-2 algorithm so harder items appear more often.

### Quiz
Five question types that test different skills:
- **Multiple Choice** — pick the correct translation
- **Translation** — type the English or Amharic equivalent
- **Listening** — identify what you hear
- **Fill in the Blank** — complete a sentence
- **Matching** — pair Amharic with English

### Speed Drill
Timed challenges for rapid recall of vocabulary and phrases.

### Sentence Builder
Construct Amharic sentences using grammar pattern templates and substitution words.
