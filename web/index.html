ፊደል · Fidel

Learn to read, write and speak Amharic.

Start with the 238 characters of the Ge'ez script, then build up words, phrases and real conversations. Native-voice audio throughout. No account, no ads, and it works offline.

Try it in your browser Get it on the App Store

Free and open source under the MIT license.

What's inside

The whole Fidel

All 238 characters with audio, and guided tracing that teaches stroke order with a forgiving, satisfying feel.

Lessons that stay open

Eight lessons from greetings to culture. Nothing is locked; a gentle recommendation picks what to do next.

Eleven ways to practice

Spaced-repetition flashcards, quizzes, fill-the-blank, word order, match pairs, reading passages and more.

  • 1,038 words
  • 706 phrases
  • 30 conversations
  • 94 grammar patterns
  • 3,320 audio clips

Private by design

Your progress stays on your device. On iPhone it can sync through your own iCloud; in the browser it lives in local storage. Export a backup from either and import it into the other to switch devices.

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Selam, an on-device tutor, answers your questions using only Fidel's own curriculum.

Help make it better

Fidel is open source. If you speak Amharic, the most valuable contribution is a correction: every word and phrase lives in plain JSON, and there's an issue form for fixes.
